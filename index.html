Our Story

Agni & Aura is more than a brand — it is a story of two women, two generations, and one shared dream.

Built by a mother and daughter, this journey began not just with candles… but with moments of togetherness, late-night ideas, quiet support, and an unspoken belief in each other.

Where one brought experience, patience, and warmth, the other brought fresh vision, creativity, and ambition. And somewhere between these two worlds, Agni & Aura was born.

“Agni”

The fire within us. The strength to start, to create, to keep going.

“Aura”

The energy we carry. The calm, the positivity, the feeling we leave behind.
